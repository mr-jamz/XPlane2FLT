# XPlane2FLT v1.0.14

- Restores v1.0.13 as the functional baseline.
- Exports every selected X-Plane OBJ as its own named OpenFlight hierarchy group.
- Prevents geometry from different source OBJ files from being merged, including when they share textures or materials.
- Generates unique eight-character OpenFlight IDs when source filenames collide.
- Leaves animation parsing, base-pose behavior, coordinates, normals, UVs, materials, textures, and flat preview lighting unchanged from v1.0.13.
